## Abstract

### The Problem

India's 260 million farmers face multiple barriers to modern agriculture:

1. **Digital Exclusion** - 65% cannot access online marketplaces due to literacy barriers
2. **Information Asymmetry** - Real-time weather and market data exists but isn't accessible
3. **Disease Losses** - ₹50,000 crore annual losses from late crop disease detection
4. **Middleman Dependency** - Farmers receive 30-40% below market price

### The Solution: AgriVox

**AgriVox** is a comprehensive AI-powered platform combining three innovative technologies:

#### 🎤 Voice AI (Multi-Language Commerce)
- 12+ Indian language support including dialects
- Natural conversation for listing produce
- Zero literacy requirement
- ONDC-compliant market access

#### 📸 Vision AI (Crop Health Detection) - *KEY DIFFERENTIATOR*
- **Technology**: Google Gemini 3.0 Flash Vision capabilities
- **Function**: Upload crop photos → Instant disease/pest detection
- **Output**: Treatment recommendations in farmer's local language
- **Impact**: Early detection can save 30-40% of crop value

```
Farmer takes photo → Gemini Vision analyzes → 
Disease identified → Treatment in Hindi/Marathi/Tamil
```

#### ☀️ Weather AI (Smart Farming Intelligence) - *KEY DIFFERENTIATOR*
- **Technology**: OpenWeatherMap API + ML predictions
- **Function**: Location-based agricultural recommendations
- **Output**: Irrigation scheduling, spraying conditions, harvest timing
- **Impact**: Optimize water usage by 20-30%

```
Location detected → Weather fetched → 
Agricultural alerts generated → Voice advisory in local language
```
